Simulator Controls & Features

Map & Navigation

  • Pan & Zoom: Click and drag the map, scroll, or use pinch-to-zoom.
  • Select Locations: Click a marker to view its catchment radius and distance lines to competitors. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple.
  • Centre Map: Use the crosshair icon in the bottom toolbar to snap back to the starting view.

Adding & Editing Locations

  • Move Locations: Drag colored markers directly on the map.
  • Add via Location: Click "+ Location" on a company card to place via a UK postcode or Lat/Lng.
  • Import CSV: Use the upload button to bulk-import locations and volumes.
  • Quick Drop: Double-click empty map space to instantly drop a new location.
  • Adjust Volume (Fast): Hover over any map marker and scroll your mouse wheel to change volume by 10.

M&A and Remedies

  • Mergers: Click the chain icon on a company card, then click another company's chain icon to merge.
  • Divestitures: Drag a location row from one company card and drop it onto another to transfer ownership.

Market Parameters (Bottom Toolbar)

  • Mode Toggle: Switch between Market Share (%) and Fascia Count (#) modes. Fascia mode evaluates the number of distinct competitors within the catchment area.
  • Threshold: Adjust the Safe Harbor threshold. Locations triggering this threshold will flag a warning and pulse red.
  • Radius (Dmax): Adjust the maximum catchment distance in Kilometers.
  • Shuffle: Generate a completely random local market scenario!

The Math of Local Overlap Analysis

In competition economics, authorities evaluate retail mergers by analyzing specific geographic catchments.

1. The Catchment Area (Isochrone)

We define a local market by a Maximum Distance (Dmax in Kilometers), representing how far a consumer is willing to travel.

2. Distance Weighting

Competitors located right next door exert massive pressure, while competitors on the edge of the catchment exert very little. We calculate real-world kilometer distance (d) using the Haversine formula, and weight each store's volume:

Weight = (Dmax - d) / Dmax

3. Calculating Market Share

To find a specific location's local market share, we take its volume, add the weighted volume of all allied stores in range, and divide it by the total weighted volume of ALL stores in range.

Share = Σ(Allied Weighted Vol) / Σ(Total Weighted Vol)

4. Fascia Counting (Alternative Mode)

Instead of calculating volume shares, this mode simply counts the number of distinct corporate entities (fascias) operating within the catchment area, including the target location. Authorities often use "4-to-3" or "3-to-2" fascia reduction screens as an initial indicator of local market concentration.
