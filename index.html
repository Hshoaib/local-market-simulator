Simulator Controls & Features

Map & Navigation

  • Pan & Zoom: Click and drag the map, scroll, or use pinch-to-zoom.
  • Select Locations: Click a marker on the map, or its row in the sidebar, to view its catchment radius and distance lines to competitors. Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) while clicking to select multiple locations at once.
  • Center Map: Use the crosshair icon in the bottom toolbar to snap back to the starting view.

Adding & Editing Locations

  • Move Locations: Click and drag the colored location markers directly on the map.
  • Add via Postcode: Click "+ Location" on a company card to place a location via a UK postcode.
  • Quick Drop: Double-click anywhere on empty map space to instantly drop a new location.
  • Adjust Volume (Fast): Hover your mouse over any map marker and scroll your mouse wheel to easily change its volume in increments of 10.
  • Manual Edits: Use the text boxes in the sidebar rows to precisely edit location names and volumes.

M&A and Remedies

  • Mergers: Click the chain icon on a company card, then click another company's chain icon to simulate a merger between them.
  • Divestitures: Click and drag a location row from one company card and drop it onto another company to transfer ownership.
  • Delete: Click the 'X' on a location row to delete it, or the 'X' on a company card to remove the entire company.

Market Parameters (Bottom Toolbar)

  • Threshold: Adjust the Safe Harbor threshold (%). Locations exceeding this market share will flag a warning and pulse red.
  • Radius (Dmax): Adjust the maximum catchment distance in Kilometers.
  • Shuffle: Generate a completely random local market scenario!

The Math of Local Overlap Analysis

In competition economics, authorities evaluate retail mergers by analyzing specific geographic catchments.

1. The Catchment Area (Isochrone)

We define a local market by a Maximum Distance (Dmax in Kilometers), representing how far a consumer is willing to travel.

2. Distance Weighting

Competitors located right next door exert massive pressure, while competitors on the edge of the catchment exert very little. We calculate real-world kilometer distance (d) using the Haversine formula, and weight each store's volume:

Weight = (Dmax - d) / Dmax

3. Calculating Market Share

To find a specific location's local market share, we take its volume, add the weighted volume of all allied stores in range, and divide it by the total weighted volume of ALL stores in range.

Share = Σ(Allied Weighted Vol) / Σ(Total Weighted Vol)
