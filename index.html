Simulator Controls & Features

Map & Navigation

  • Pan & Zoom: Click and drag the map, scroll, or use pinch-to-zoom.
  • Select Locations: Click a marker or its sidebar row to view its catchment and the distance (or travel time) to competitors. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple.
  • Centre Map: Use the crosshair icon in the bottom toolbar to snap back to the starting view.

Adding & Editing Locations

  • Move Locations: Drag coloured markers directly on the map.
  • Add via Location: Click "+ Location" on a company card to place via a UK postcode or Lat/Lng.
  • Import CSV: Use the upload button to bulk-import locations and volumes.
  • Quick Drop: Double-click empty map space to instantly drop a new location.
  • Adjust Volume (Fast): Hover over any map marker and scroll your mouse wheel to change volume by 10, or edit the number in the sidebar.

Company Cards

  • Rename: Double-click a company name.
  • Reorder: Use the arrows beside the name to move a card up or down.
  • Colour: Click the colour swatch beside the name.

M&A and Remedies

  • Mergers: Click the chain icon on a company card, then click another company's chain icon to merge.
  • Divestitures: Drag a location row from one company card and drop it onto another to transfer ownership.

Market Parameters (Bottom Toolbar)

  • Mode Toggle: Switch between Market Share (%) and Fascia Count (#) modes. Fascia mode evaluates the number of distinct competitors within the catchment area.
  • Threshold: Adjust the Safe Harbor threshold. Locations triggering this threshold will flag a warning and pulse red.
  • Radius / Time: Adjust the catchment: kilometres for straight-line distance, or minutes for travel time.
  • Shuffle: Generate a completely random local market scenario!

Travel-Time Catchments

By default catchments use straight-line distance. To use real drive, walk or cycle times:

  • In Settings, tick "Use drive/walk/cycle time" and paste a free OpenRouteService API key.
  • New, moved or imported locations (and all locations after switching travel mode) show Pending… (and "?" in the sidebar) until travel times are fetched. Click the refresh button at the end of the bottom toolbar; its badge shows how many locations need updating.
  • Changing the time limit (up to 60 minutes) reuses travel times already fetched, so results update instantly. Catchment outlines are fetched for nearby limits too; if one isn't loaded yet, a selected location shows a dashed circle until the next refresh.
  • Large refreshes are paced to the free plan's per-minute limits. Click the button again to stop; results so far are kept.
  • Locations with no road within ~350 m for the chosen travel mode show N/A.

The Math of Local Overlap Analysis

In competition economics, authorities evaluate retail mergers by analyzing specific geographic catchments.

1. The Catchment Area (Isochrone)

We define a local market by a maximum distance (Dmax in kilometres) or, with travel-time catchments enabled, a maximum travel time (in minutes), representing how far a consumer is willing to travel.

2. Distance Weighting

Competitors located right next door exert massive pressure, while competitors on the edge of the catchment exert very little. We use straight-line distance (d) from the Haversine formula, or the travel time from OpenRouteService, and weight each store's volume:

Weight = (Dmax - d) / Dmax

A store is always 0 distance from itself, so its own volume has full weight.

3. Calculating Market Share

To find a specific location's local market share, we take its volume, add the weighted volume of all allied stores in range, and divide it by the total weighted volume of ALL stores in range. With weighting disabled, every weight is 1.

Share = Σ(Allied Weighted Vol) / Σ(Total Weighted Vol)

4. Fascia Counting (Alternative Mode)

Instead of calculating volume shares, this mode simply counts the number of distinct corporate entities (fascias) operating within the catchment area, including the target location. Authorities often use "4-to-3" or "3-to-2" fascia reduction screens as an initial indicator of local market concentration.
